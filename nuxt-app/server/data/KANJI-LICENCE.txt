kanji.json is derived from two files published by the Electronic Dictionary
Research and Development Group (EDRDG):

  KANJIDIC2   https://www.edrdg.org/kanjidic/kanjidic2.xml.gz
  KRADFILE    http://ftp.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/kradzip.zip

Both are copyright the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group
and are used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence
(https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html). KRADFILE is also copyright
Michael Raine and James Breen.

Changes: only the fields this app uses were kept (English meanings, on and kun
readings, stroke count, grade, JLPT level, frequency rank, the Heisig frame
number, and KRADFILE components), and kanji KRADFILE does not decompose were
dropped. Regenerate with:

  bun run scripts/build-kanji-data.ts <kanjidic2.xml> <kradfile>
